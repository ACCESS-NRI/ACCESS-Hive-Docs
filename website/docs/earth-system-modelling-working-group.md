# Earth System Modelling

The **Earth System Modelling (ESM) Working Group** includes researchers from many Australian universities and research institutions who use and co-develop the Australian Community Climate Earth System Model (ACCESS) for their research.

![](wp-content/uploads/2023/08/ACCESS-icon-EARTH-SYS-MOD-300x300.png)

The focus is on [ACCESS-CM](models/access-cm.md) and ACCESS-ESM models as well as anything related to paleoclimate or fully coupled climate modelling.

[How to join a Community Working Group](join-community-working-groups.md){ .buttononblue }

[Earth System WG Home](https://forum.access-hive.org.au/c/esm/esm-working-group/43){ .buttononblue }

Click on the Earth System WG Home button above to follow this working group mailing list, information about events and meetings and activity reports.

Initially the aims of this group are to

- exchange information though regular meetings
- act as a conduit between the community and the ACCESS-NRI Scientific Advisory Committee and ACCESS-NRI
- co-develop the models
- facilitate collaboration on shared projects
