# CMIP7 Location tips and how to get there

**CSIRO Aspendale information**

Address: 107-121 Station Street, Aspendale, Vic 3195. 

Reception: 03 9239 4400 

- The site is on the corner of Robertson Parade and access to the parking area at the front of the site is off Robertson Parade. If the front carpark is full, there is another carpark at the back of the site with an entrance further along Robertson Parade. It has a gate but there is an intercom to request reception to open the gate for you.
- The Aspendale site is a short work from Aspendale Station, on the Frankston train line. Public transport information is available here: [Journey planner – Public Transport Victoria](https://transport.vic.gov.au/journey). You will require a Myki card and to touch on/touch off at the start/end of your journey. Travel from the airport requires taking the SkyBus to Southern Cross station and picking up the Frankston line there.
- CSIRO requires all visitors to sign-in using our Envoy system. You will receive an email on Monday with details on how to fill out most of the sign in information ahead of time. This is best done on a phone as the process will generate a QR code which you can then use for the final sign in step when you arrive. If you aren’t able to sign-in ahead of time, this can be done at the Envoy terminal when you arrive. The sign-in is only valid for one day, so a second email will be sent to you on Tuesday for attending on-site on Wednesday.
