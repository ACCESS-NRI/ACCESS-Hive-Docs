# Community input invited: ACCESS-NRI Annual Work Plan (FY2026-27)

*March 12, 2026*

**ACCESS-NRI is commencing its planning cycle to develop our next Annual Work Plan (FY2026-27), and we are keen to involve the community more closely in this process.**

This year, we are introducing multiple opportunities for community input as the work plan is developed and refined. Feedback will be sought at three key stages, allowing ideas and priorities to be shared early, and reviewed as the plan takes shape. 

**![](wp-content/uploads/2026/02/DSC00688-300x200.jpg){ .align-right }Key dates for community input and feedback** 

- **5 February – 16 March**: Online form opens to collect early input and feedback to inform drafting of the new work plan.

- **April**: Community feedback on the initial draft of the work plan.

- **Mid-late May**: Final community review (for minor updates only).

**The [online form](https://forms.office.com/r/y8d8LSY1Vi) for early input is now open** and closes on **16 March, 2026**. To help inform your feedback, you may wish to refer to our  [current-year status report (FY2025-2026)](wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Status-Work-Plan-FY2025-26-Feb-2026.pdf).

We encourage community members to share their ideas, priorities and suggestions via this form. Early feedback will help inform the initial drafting of the work plan.

We look forward to working with the community to shape priorities for the coming year.

For any questions, please contact [access.nri@anu.edu.au](mailto:access.nri@anu.edu.au).
