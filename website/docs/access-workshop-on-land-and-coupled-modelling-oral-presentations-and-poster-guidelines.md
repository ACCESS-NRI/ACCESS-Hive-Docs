# ACCESS Workshop on Land and Coupled Modelling: Oral presentations and Poster Guidelines

# **Presenting at the workshop**

## Presentation types

The Program contains a mix of the following presentation types:  

- Talks 15 minutes (including questions) in plenary of parallel sessions.
- Posters (may include lightning talk 1 min)

#### **Poster Display**

These includes a 1 min lightning presentation. 

Lightning talk presentations will be 1 minute in length for a **single presentation slide**. Creativity is encouraged, and there will be prizes for Most Creative Lightning Talks and Best poster. The idea of the lightning talks is to introduce your poster in a 1 minute 1 slide presentation, to encourage longer discussions in front of your poster during the poster sessions.

The poster must not exceed the size of A0 (1189-841mm) and be brought along to the Workshop for display. **Portrait format is preferred** to landscape, as our poster boards are slightly smaller than A0 landscape.

All posters will be displayed for the duration of the Workshop. Poster presenters are required to attend the poster sessions.  

- Poster set-up time: Monday 31 August, 9:15–9:45 am. before the start of the workshop.
- Poster pack-up: Wednesday 2 September, before afternoon tea

Before the start of the Workshop, you will receive an email with instructions for posters/lightning talks. We will have pins and poster numbers ready to go at the venue.
