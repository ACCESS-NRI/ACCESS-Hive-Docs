# What are climate models? Watch our new animation!

*August 25, 2026*

What are climate models, and who is behind the models that inform Australia’s understanding of its own weather and climate?

As National Science Week 2026 draws to a close, we are releasing this new animation that explains what climate models are and why we can’t navigate a changing climate without them. A climate model is not a guess. It is millions of equations, run on some of Australia’s most powerful supercomputers, simulating the atmosphere, oceans, land, and ice and biological components together.

[![What are climate models?](wp-content/uploads/2026/08/what-are-climate-models-V1-570x320.png)](https://youtu.be/tK0tolvqONc "Watch What are climate models?")

### What are climate models?

Ever wondered what a “climate model” actually is?
Our new animation explains why we need climate models, how these models work, and how ACCESS-NRI supports the Australian research community building and using them.

[Watch Video](https://youtu.be/tK0tolvqONc "Watch What are climate models?"){ .buttononblue }

This animation shines a light on the value of collaboration between two of the NCRIS facilities that make climate research possible: ACCESS-NRI, Australia’s climate simulator, and NCI’s Gadi supercomputer, which powers the climate simulations run by ACCESS-NRI and the Australian research community.

Another clear example of the enormous global and local impact of the National Collaborative Research Infrastructure Strategy (NCRIS).
