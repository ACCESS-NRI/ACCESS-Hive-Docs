# Welcome to our new Release Management Team Leader, Dr Aidan Heerdegen

*August 5, 2022*

Dr Aidan Heerdegen has been appointed Team leader of the **Model Release Management Team**, which is responsible for releasing supported model software configurations for use by the Australian Climate and Weather Community.

At the ACCESS-NRI, Aidan will help researchers improve their productivity through new and improved tools.
