# ACCESS Community Workshop 2023 (5-6 September)

*September 5, 2023*

**This annual event brings together the community of users, developers and collaborators of the ACCESS Earth system models.  Everyone is welcome, including ACCESS developers, users of ACCESS model output, people considering using ACCESS in the future and those at all career stages.**

**![](../wp-content/uploads/2023/07/ACCESS-Workshop-20232-300x169.png){ .align-left }When:**  5-6 of September 2023. This is one of several events occurring during the week (see the related events below). We encourage you to also register for these other events and stay for the whole week.

**Where:** Canberra, ACT on Ngunnawal and Ngambri country and online from 5-6 of September 2023.

**More information:** [../access-community-workshop-2023.md](../access-community-workshop-2023.md)
