# Scientific Python Conference 2024

*July 8, 2024 - July 14, 2024*

SciPy brings together attendees from industry, academia and government to showcase their latest projects, learn from skilled users and developers, and collaborate on code development.

## Conference details

**When:** July 8-14, 2024

**Where:** Tacoma, WA (USA) & Online

**More details**: [SciPy 2024 website](https://www.scipy2024.scipy.org/)  or check out the ACCESS-Hive forum post here: <https://forum.access-hive.org.au/t/scipy-2024-submit-for-a-talk-or-poster-presentation/1838>.

## What is SciPy?

If you’re new to SciPy, it is short for “Scientific Python”, which includes the use of the Python computing language for any scientific discipline. The conference is a mix of people with research backgrounds (academic/government) with those working closer to the tech industry in data science and software engineering. It’s a very welcoming and friendly community, and it would be fun to see more Australian representation there!

The conference has three portions, totaling 7 days (and you can attend any or all portions):

- *Monday & Tuesday* – 4 x half-day tutorials
- *Wednesday, Thursday, & Friday* – 3-day conference with oral and poster sessions, keynotes, and birds of a feather (similar to town hall) sessions
- *Saturday & Sunday* – Sprints! Informal hacking time!

You can view [last year’s schedule](https://www.scipy2023.scipy.org/schedule/#2023-07-12) to get a sense of the types of sessions and presentations at SciPy! (Scroll down to view Wednesday – Friday sessions.)

*(NOTE: this is **not** a conference about the scipy software package! It is confusing naming, but the conference includes any and all Python packages used in the sciences, which includes the scipy package, as well as many, many others!).*

## Submit for a talk or poster!

[**Call for proposals**](https://cfp.scipy.org/2024/cfp) (due Feb. 27th)
You can submit for a talk or poster to one of 8 tracks. Of particular interest to this community is likely the *Earth, Ocean, Geo, and Atmospheric Science track* (that I am co-chairing! ![:tada:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/tada.png?v=12)). All tracks are listed on the “Call for proposals” page linked above.

### <a id="review-process-2"></a>Review process

All presentations will be openly reviewed by 3 reviewers and session chairs will base their acceptance decisions on these reviews. If accepted, presenters have the option of writing up a short paper that will be published with DOI in the SciPy proceedings. *NOTE: oral presenters **must attend in-person**. Posters can be given remotely.*
