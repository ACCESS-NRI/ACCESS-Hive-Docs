# Australian Antarctic Research Conference

*November 19, 2024 - November 22, 2024*

Connecting Australia’s polar research Community in a rapidly changing world

**Themes:**

- Antarctica in a changing climate and Earth system
- Antarctic extremes and beyond
- Antarctica’s past
- Human dimension of Antarctica
- Antarctica’s future: Research directions in the global context
- Regime shifts and tipping points in the Antarctic system
- Other Antarctic/Southern Ocean/Sub-Antarctic topics

**Important dates:**

- Abstract submissions closed
- Registrations open until **Monday 21 October 2024**

**Where:** Hobart, Tasmania

**When:** Tuesday 18 November- Friday 22 November, ECR day on Monday 18 November

**More information**: https://antarctic.org.au/2024-conference/
