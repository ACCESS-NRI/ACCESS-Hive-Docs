# 2023 ANU Research Infrastructure Expo

*November 28, 2023*

Join us and many other facilities for an exciting event showcasing of the state-of-the-art research infrastructure that underpins research excellence at ANU!

ANU hosts a world-leading landscape of research infrastructure. This ranges from world-class physical research facilities and networks of leading expertise, to highly skilled lab technicians and dedicated research data service teams. The 2023 Research Infrastructure Expo is an exciting opportunity for university staff, researchers, students, government and industry partners to discover the diverse research infrastructure at ANU and engage with the amazing work happening in the ANU research infrastructure landscape.

**When:** Tue, 28 Nov 2023 1:00 PM – 4:00 PM AEDT

**Where:** Level 2. Marie Reay Teaching Centre 155 University Avenue, Kambri ANU Canberra, ACT 2601

[Register](https://www.eventbrite.com.au/e/anu-research-infrastructure-expo-2023-tickets-650573070567){ .buttononblue }

**Who’s welcome?**

Featuring exhibits from all disciplines, the Expo is open to the entire ANU community and external partners. Come and explore the latest advancements in research infrastructure and be inspired!
