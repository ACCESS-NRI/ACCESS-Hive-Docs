# Australia’s contribution to the World Climate Research Program km-scale Global Hackathon

*July 24, 2025 - July 24, 2025*

In May, the [ARC Centre of Excellence for 21st Century Weather](https://www.21centuryweather.org.au), in partnership with **NRI** and **ACCESS NRI**, led the Australian node of the **World Climate Research Program (WCRP) km-scale Hackathon**.

This seminar will provide an overview of the hackathon’s science, logistics, and technical development, followed by a short presentation from each of the ten science projects sharing their experience and findings.

**About the Hackathon**

The hackathon brought together over 600 hundred scientists from 10 global nodes to analyse output from multiple climate models simulating the annual cycle at sub 5 km resolution, with a goal of sharing best practices and encouraging global data sharing and collaboration.

**About the Graduate Colloquium**

The Australian node of the Hackathon formed the 2025 Graduate Colloquium of the ARC Centre of Excellence for Weather of the 21st Century.

The Graduate Colloquium is a cornerstone of 21st Century Weather’s Training & Leadership Program.

We want graduate students to gain highly specialised knowledge in their own area of research, as well as a broad understanding of the wider discipline. These events provide this opportunity.
