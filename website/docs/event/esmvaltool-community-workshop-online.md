# ESMValTool Community Workshop (Online)

*November 19, 2024 - November 21, 2024*

We’re excited to announce the upcoming ESMValTool Community Workshop, which will take place *fully online from November 19th to 21st*. This workshop offers a fantastic opportunity to connect with fellow ESMValTool users and developers, share insights, and work together to enhance our workflows.

**Workshop Details:**

- **Date:** November 19-21
- **Format:** Online
- **Registration Link:** [Register here](https://aus01.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fforms.office.com%2Fe%2F2x34zAn2tD&data=05%7C02%7CNatalia.Bateman%40anu.edu.au%7C7bb241d5008c4ecbb86608dcfeb6dd68%7Ce37d725cab5c46249ae5f0533e486437%7C0%7C0%7C638665308553936061%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=F%2Bz8vxJlbSXY%2B6%2Bq3xfRqhaHHyqGCvDsNlq5plfuWeA%3D&reserved=0)

Our goal is to split the workshop time evenly between **discussions** and **collaborative coding** sessions. To ensure the agenda meets everyone’s needs, we invite you to share your interests and ideas for both parts. Please leave a comment in the discussion thread [here](https://aus01.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2FESMValGroup%2FCommunity%2Fdiscussions%2F192&data=05%7C02%7CNatalia.Bateman%40anu.edu.au%7C7bb241d5008c4ecbb86608dcfeb6dd68%7Ce37d725cab5c46249ae5f0533e486437%7C0%7C0%7C638665308553953952%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=Nu%2BITsUKb0y2TCw06XMPNxz8xyr2PRiBv3YvtC%2BsJcA%3D&reserved=0) indicating specific topics or features you would like us to cover.

More information in the ACCESS-Hive Forum: <https://forum.access-hive.org.au/t/upcoming-esmvaltool-community-workshop-online-19-21-november-2024/3882>

**How You Can Help Shape the Agenda:**

- Share your interests for discussion topics.
- Suggest tools, workflows, or features to explore during the coding sessions.

We look forward to your input and to seeing many of you there!

Thank you!
