# GEWEX Open Science Conference

*July 7, 2024 - July 12, 2024*

This conference will celebrate 30+ years of GEWEX research, the strong role of the Japanese research community and set the stage for the next phase of research addressing the [World Climate Research Programme (WCRP)](https://www.wcrp-climate.org/) main challenges on water resources, extremes and climate sensitivity through observations and data sets, their analyses, process studies, model development and exploitation, applications, technology transfer to operational use, and research capacity development and training for the next generation of scientists worldwide.

**When:**7-12 July, 2024, **Abstract deadline**:1 Feb 2024

**Where:**Sapporo, Japan

[Registration and more information](https://www.gewexevents.org/meetings/gewex-osc2024/)
