# AMOS Conference 2025 – including ACCESS-NRI workshops

*June 23, 2025 - June 27, 2025*

The Australian Meteorological and Oceanographic Society (AMOS) is Australia’s pre-eminent meeting profiling the latest findings and applications of meteorology, climate science and oceanography across local, national and global scales.

**What:** 31th AMOS Conference 2025

**When:** 23-27 June 2025

**Where:** Cairns Convention Centre, QLDCanberra Hyatt. Our pre-conference workshops will take place on Monday 5 February 2024 at ANU (TBC).

**More information and to register:** https://www.eventcreate.com/e/amos2024

As part of the AMOS Conference ACCESS-NRI is hosting to Climate Model Workshops:

AMOS Registration is required to access the two separate registration forms for these workshops:

**Workshop 1: An overview of climate modeling, focused on Australia’s climate models (ACCESS)**

*![](../wp-content/uploads/2024/05/ACCESS-Logo-Just-planet-RGB-300x197.jpg){ .align-left }Tuesday, June 24 @ 7:30 – 9:00am*

Whether you’re curious about how climate models work, interested in the Australian ACCESS model suite, or exploring how climate modeling might support your own research, this session will give you the foundational knowledge and confidence to take your first steps into the world of climate modeling. No prior knowledge required!

**Workshop 2: Working With Australia’s Climate Model Output Data: First Steps for Researchers**

*![](../wp-content/uploads/2024/05/ACCESS-Logo-Just-planet-RGB-300x197.jpg){ .align-left }Wednesday, June 25 @ 7:30-9:00am*

This session will give you the practical tools and confidence to take your first steps toward using climate model output—and know where to go to learn more. It will be geared toward those who already have a general understanding of what climate models are, but no specific background is required.
