# ACCESS Community Visualisation Webinar Series: historical sea-ice around Antarctica

*March 21, 2025 - March 21, 2025*

We are excited to invite you to the second event in our **ACCESS Community Visualisation Webinar Series**, following the success of our first session in December. Join us on **Friday, 21st March at 11am** (AEDT) for an engaging webinar exploring *“historical sea-ice around Antarctica”*.

This session will showcase the *sea-ice Jupyter notebook* from the [ACCESS-Visualisation Recipes](https://github.com/ACCESS-NRI/ACCESS-Visualisation-Recipes), developed by Owen Kaluza (ACCESS-NRI), which enables users to *reproduce or create similar visualisations* to those featured in [Visualising Antarctica’s Heartbeat](../visualising-antarcticas-heartbeat.md) and [A Visual Expedition to Antarctica’s Past](../a-visual-expedition-to-antarcticas-past.md). These visualisations provide unique insights into seasonal and historical sea-ice variability.

#### <a id="p-13619-webinar-highlights-2"></a>Webinar Highlights

![:triangular_flag_on_post:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/triangular_flag_on_post.png?v=14) **Live Demonstration**: *Max Proft* and *Rhaegar Zeng* from the Model Evaluation and Diagnostics Team at ACCESS-NRI will walk through the sea-ice Jupyter notebook.

![:triangular_flag_on_post:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/triangular_flag_on_post.png?v=14) **New `accessvis` functionalities**: Discover recent enhancements to the [*accessvis*](https://github.com/ACCESS-NRI/ACCESS-Vis/tree/main) Python package for Earth system data visualisation.

![:triangular_flag_on_post:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/triangular_flag_on_post.png?v=14) **Hands-on Exploration**: Participate in guided exercises to integrate these tools into your research workflow.

![:triangular_flag_on_post:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/triangular_flag_on_post.png?v=14) **Interactive Q&A**: Ask questions, share feedback, and engage with experts.
