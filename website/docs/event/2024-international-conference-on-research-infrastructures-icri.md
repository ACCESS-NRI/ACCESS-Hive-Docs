# 2024 International Conference on Research Infrastructures (ICRI)

*December 3, 2024 - December 5, 2024*

**Join policymakers, research institution leaders, facility operator, users and researchers at the 2024 International Conference on Research Infrastructures (ICRI 2024) in Brisbane**

The 2024 International Conference on Research Infrastructures (ICRI 2024) will be hosted in Meanjin/Brisbane, Australia.

Hosted biennially, the ICRI is the major professional forum for the global research infrastructure community. ICRI 2024 will bring together policymakers, research institution leaders, facility operator, users and researchers, to discuss pressing topics of interest, trends and challenges facing research infrastructures around the world.

ICRI 2024 will be delivered by CSIRO, Australia’s national science agency.

More information about ICRI 2024 is coming soon. To say updated, visit the [ICRI 2024 website](https://icri2024.au/).
