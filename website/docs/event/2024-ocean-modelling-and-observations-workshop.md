# 2024 Ocean Modelling and Observations Workshop

*July 2, 2024 - July 4, 2024*

The 2024 Ocean Modelling and Observations Workshop is a joint workshop of the [Consortium for Ocean-Sea Ice Modelling in Australia](https://cosima.org.au/) (COSIMA) & Australian Coastal and Oceans Modelling & Observations (ACOMO).

The workshop is brought to you by [IMOS](https://imos.org.au/) and [ACCESS-NRI,](../index.md) which are enabled by the Australian Government through the National Collaborative Research Infrastructure Strategy (NCRIS).

##### **Workshop details:**

**When:** 2nd-4th July, sessions will run from 9 am-5 pm each day.

**Where:** Marie Reay Teaching Centre (Building 155), Kambri on the Australian National University campus

**Please note on the first day of the workshop:**

**8.00 am** Registration opens at Level 6 of the Marie Reay Teaching Centre
**8.50 am** Please be seated in the Plenary room on Level 6 for the Welcome to Country and Introduction.

##### **Contact** Should you require any assistance in the lead up to the conference, please email Richard Saunders at: Richard.Saunders(at)utas.edu.au.
