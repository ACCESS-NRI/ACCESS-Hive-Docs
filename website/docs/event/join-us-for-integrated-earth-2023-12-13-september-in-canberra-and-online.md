# Join us for Integrated Earth 2023, 12-13 September in Canberra and online

*September 12, 2023*

##### Integrated Earth 2023 is a two-day conference between 12-13 September at the Shine Dome on Ngunnawal and Ngambri Country (Canberra) for scientists and STEM professionals to find ways to better integrate data across each of the Earth systems — geosphere, biosphere, cryosphere, hydrosphere and atmosphere — to address complex Earth systems challenges.

##### ![](../wp-content/uploads/2023/07/BannerIE2023-300x160.png){ .align-left }

**Where:** Shine Dome, Australian Academy of Science, Canberra and online

**When:** 12-13 September, 2023

**Expressions of interest closing** on 12 July, 2023

**More information and to register**: <https://www.tern.org.au/integrated-earth/>
