# CMIP Community Workshop 2026

*March 9, 2026 - March 13, 2026*

![](../wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Event-Photo-CMIP2026-1024x307.png){ .align-center }

**Location: *Kyoto, Japan***

The CMIP Community Workshop 2026 will bring together a growing, and increasingly diverse, community of Earth system scientists, partners, and stakeholders. We seek to build on the popularity of the Barcelona CMIP6 workshop by incorporating its most successful elements while ensuring that this meeting aligns with WCRP hybrid meeting requirements and remains inclusive of the ever-expanding CMIP community.
