# ACCESS Community Working Group (WG) Workshops

*September 7, 2023*

You are invited to register to the **ACCESS Community Working Group (WG) Workshops**, which are happening over 1or 2 days after our main Workshop.

**When:** 1 or 2 days (7-8 September, 2023)

**Where:** [J.G. Crawford Building (Building 132)](https://www.google.com.au/maps/place/Crawford+School+of+Public+Policy/@-35.2875508,149.114169,17z/data=!3m1!5s0x6b164d39d1a63eb9:0x258a0d0115b412a0!4m6!3m5!1s0x6b164d39dfe929a5:0x735cf0299faa6ed7!8m2!3d-35.287434!4d149.1161406!16s%2Fm%2F065yk8b?entry=ttu), Lennox Crossing, ANU, Canberra (in person) and online though Zoom. Online attendance options will be provided, but we can’t guarantee a full hybrid engagement participation in breakout rooms, so for a better experience we encourage you to participate in person.

- Atmospheric Modelling WG Workshop (7 September) [Connect with this WG](https://forum.access-hive.org.au/c/atmosphere/7)
- Earth System Modelling WG Workshop (7 September) [Connect with this WG](https://forum.access-hive.org.au/c/esm/35)
- Forecasting and Prediction WG Workshop (7 September) [Connect with this WG](https://forum.access-hive.org.au/c/forecasting-and-prediction/36)
- Land Surface Modelling WG Workshop (7-8 September) [Connect with this WG](https://forum.access-hive.org.au/c/land/8)
- COSIMA -Ocean and Sea-Ice WG Workshop (7-8 September) [Connect with this WG](https://forum.access-hive.org.au/c/cosima/29)
- Cryosphere (7 September) [Connect with this WG](https://forum.access-hive.org.au/c/cryosphere/34)

For people attending in person: All breaks and lunch are included and will happen on the same location/time allowing participants to attend different workshops and interact with other WGs

Please note that this event may be recorded and published by the ACCESS-NRI through its communication channels. This may include your contributions during the sessions.
