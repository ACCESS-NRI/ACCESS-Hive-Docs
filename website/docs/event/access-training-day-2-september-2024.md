# ACCESS Training Day 2024

*September 2, 2024 - September 2, 2024*

ACCESS-NRI, Australia’s Climate Simulator,  would like to invite you to the **2024 ACCESS Training Day.**

[Register here](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/access-training-day-2024-tickets-948535975637){ .buttononblue }

**When:** September 2, 2024. We encourage you to also register for the [ACCESS Community Workshop 2024](../access-community-workshop-2024.md) and stay for the whole week.

**Where:** This face-to-face event will be held in Canberra, ACT on Ngunnawal and Ngambri country. Level 5, Marie Reay Teaching Centre, The Australian National University (ANU).

**Who should attend?** Anyone is welcome to attend, but registration is required. Registrations close on 23 August 2024.

---

**Accessibility**

We are committed to making this event accessible to everyone. If there any specific access requirements that we can provide to enable you to attend, please contact us at: [access.nri@anu.edu.au](mailto:access.nri@anu.edu.au).

We are aware that an in-person event precludes participation from those unable to travel to the event. We are hosting the Training Day as in-person only in order to maximise the opportunity for interaction. However, all materials will be posted online and shared with the community, and virtual training events on various topics of interest to the community will be held at other times to accommodate the entire ACCESS community.

We acknowledge COVID-19 is still active in the community. Check out our efforts to minimise the risk of COVID19 infection in all our events.
