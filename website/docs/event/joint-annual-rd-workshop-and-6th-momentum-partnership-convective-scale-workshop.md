# Joint Annual R&D Workshop and 6th Momentum Partnership Convective Scale Workshop

*September 9, 2024 - September 13, 2024*

The 2024 Bureau of Meteorology Annual Research and Development Workshop will be a special workshop held jointly with the 6th Momentum®UK Partnership Convective Scale Workshop. The hybrid workshop will be held in Melbourne 9 – 13 September 2024 and will be focused on the theme of ‘Advancing Convective Scale Predictions’.

**About the theme: Advancing convective scale predictions**

Society’s vulnerability to weather and climate hazards is rising, highlighting the need to improve predictive capabilities. Enhanced convective scale models, offering superior resolution and convective process representation, are crucial for more precise forecasts. These models are particularly important for severe weather, such as thunderstorms and flash floods. The workshop’s focus on advancing convective scale modelling aligns with the increasing demand for accuracy, which is essential for safeguarding the public and aiding government, industry, and environmental protection decision-making.

**Partnership for progress**

The Bureau and Momentum®UK Partners focus on providing targeted information and services that empower decision-making in the face of meteorological hazards. Through collaborative efforts, we co-develop essential modelling science to meet societal needs and address common challenges.

More information, including the program outline and dates for poster abstract submission and registration: <http://www.bom.gov.au/research/workshop/2024/index.shtml>
