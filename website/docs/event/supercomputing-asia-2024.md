# Supercomputing Asia 2024

*February 19, 2024 - February 22, 2024*

For the first time in its history, Supercomputing Asia is leaving Singapore and coming to Australia. SCA24 aims to promote a vibrant and shared high-performance computing and Artificial Intelligence ecosystem, for both the public and private sectors, in Asia.

**When:**19-22 February

**Where:** International Convention Centre, Sydney, NSW, 2000, Australia

**More information and registraton**: https://sca24.sc-asia.org/

Co-organised by leading supercomputing centres of the region including those in Australia, Japan, Singapore, Thailand and New Zealand, and anchored by the [National Computational Infrastructure](https://nci.org.au) in Australia.
