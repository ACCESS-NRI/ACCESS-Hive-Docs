# ACCESS-NRI participation: COSIMA and e-research

*November 15, 2022*

The ACCESS-NRI had a busy October participating in several events including:

#### **E-research Australasia 2022**

![](wp-content/uploads/2022/11/P_20221020_101042-300x225.jpg){ .align-left }e-Research Australia took place from the 17-20 October in at the Brisbane Convention and Exhibition Centre.

ACCESS-NRI participated as an exhibitor along with other NCRIS facilities including Atlas of Living Australia, Auscope, Australian ACCESS Federation, ARCD, AARNET and AURIN.

![](wp-content/uploads/2022/11/P_20221018_123700-300x225.jpg){ .align-right }

Our Associate Director, Dr Kelsey Druken presented on: “Introducing the Australian Earth System Simulator (ACCESS-NRI)” and also co-facilitated the Birds of Feather (BoF) section “Integrating research data across our planet: Perspectives from the data components of the NCRIS Earth  & Environmental science facilities” with Dr Rebecca Farrington from Auscope.

We also participated on the BoF session “Communicating across Research ecosystems” facilitated by Aditi Subramanya (Pawsey) and Jana Makar (NESI)

#### **COSIMA Workshop 2022**

![](wp-content/uploads/2022/11/COSIMA-Workshop-300x225.jpg){ .align-right }The [COSIMA](http://cosima.org.au) workshop was held in Hobart between on the 3-4 November, 2022. The workshop featured our usual mix of talks and discussions, covering physical oceanography, sea ice, biogeochemistry, waves, model development and technical advances. [More details here](http://cosima.org.au/index.php/meetings/cosima2022/)

ACCESS-NRI Director Andy Hogg gave a talk on the [ACCESS-NRI and relationship to COSIMA.](http://cosima.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Andy-Hogg-COSIMA-ACCESS-NRI-V2-2022-11-01-1541.pdf) Other participants from the ACCESS-NRI team included [Dougie Squire](person/dougie-squire.md) and [Romain Beucher](person/romain-beucher.md).
