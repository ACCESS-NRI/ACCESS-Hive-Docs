# ACCESStory newsletter

![](../wp-content/uploads/2024/11/ACCESStoryHEADER-300x169.png)

## About ACCESStory

*ACCESStory* is ACCESS-NRI’s monthly, community-driven newsletter. It includes news, opportunities, publications, and events relevant to the ACCESS community. The newsletter also highlights the latest ACCESS-NRI releases for software, tools, data and training materials.

Read the most recent *ACCESStory* newsletter below:

[**ACCESStory: Issue 23 – September 2026**](https://mailchi.mp/0eec276644e0/accesstory-issue-23-september){ .buttononblue }

#### Subscribe

Receive ACCESS-NRI newsletters and updates directly to your inbox by [subscribing here](https://access-nri.us14.list-manage.com/subscribe?u=2e82a242a5023d286ad0d4555&id=aed3bbbb13) or filling in the “News and updates” fields at the bottom of this webpage. Please note that ACCESS-NRI is hosted by the Australian National University (ANU) and abides by the [ANU Privacy Policy](https://policies.anu.edu.au/ppl/document/ANUP_010007).

#### Contribute

*ACCESStory* is a community-driven newsletter, and we welcome your contributions. If you have something that other members of the ACCESS community would find relevant, send us an email at [access.nri@anu.edu.au](mailto:access.nri@anu.edu.au).

Content that you could submit includes:

- news or updates from ACCESS-NRI partners, Community Working Groups, or related projects
- recent papers that use ACCESS models, tools, or data
- jobs or events relevant to climate/weather science or modelling
- any other content relevant to the ACCESS community.
