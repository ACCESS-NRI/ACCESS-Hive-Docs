# ACCESS-NRI Board

## About the Board

ACCESS-NRI operates under the operational framework of its host institution, The Australian National University (ANU), on the advice of the ACCESS-NRI Board and the [Scientific Advisory Committee (SAC)](scientific-advisory-committee-sac.md).

#### Approach

Our approach to governance is grounded in a commitment to integrity, accountability, and transparency. We recognise that effective governance is essential to achieving our purpose and maintaining the trust of the ACCESS climate community.

#### Composition

The ACCESS-NRI Board comprises an independent chair and several independent members, the ACCESS-NRI director, and a representative from each of the ACCESS-NRI [partner organisations](partners-and-collaborators.md). These partner organisations include: The Australian National University, Bureau of Meteorology, CSIRO, Monash University, University of Melbourne, UNSW, University of Tasmania, and the Australian Antarctic Division. Independent observers include the Department of Education (DoE)/NCRIS, and the Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water (DCCEW).

#### Meetings and agenda

The Board meets quarterly in March, June, September and December. Agenda items can be emailed to the Director, Business Manager or Board Chair via [access.nri@anu.edu.au](mailto:access.nri@anu.edu.au). The agenda is distributed to the Board, along with Board papers, 1–2 weeks before the meeting.
