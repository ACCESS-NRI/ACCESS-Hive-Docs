# Scientific Advisory Committee (SAC)

**ACCESS-NRI operates under the operational framework of its host institution, The Australian National University (ANU) on the advice of the ACCESS-NRI Board and the Scientific Advisory Committee (SAC).**

The ACCESS Scientific Advisory Committee (SAC) is formed by representatives from different organisations and scientific expertise, the ACCESS-NRI Director and a member of the ACCESS-NRI Business team in a secretariat role. It supports the Board and provide scientific advice to the infrastructure. The SAC meetings’ minutes are publicly available [here](../minutes-of-sac-meetings.md).
