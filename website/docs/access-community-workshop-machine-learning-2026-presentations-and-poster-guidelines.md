# ACCESS Workshop Connecting Machine Learning to Earth System Science: Oral presentations and Poster Guidelines

# **Presenting at the workshop**

## Presentation types

#### **Keynote talks**

30 min plenary talk, including questions.

#### **Talks (in plenary or parallel sessions)**

10-15 minutes including questions.To help maximise interaction during the session you are presenting in, we encourage you to consider limiting your talk to 10 minutes, leaving 5 minutes of time for questions and discussion to foster more interaction. Thanks for working with us to keep the sessions lively and engaging.

There is a prize for Best student presentation in this category.

#### **Poster display**  **+ 1 min lightning talk**

These will include a 1 min lightning presentation during one of the parallel or plenary sessions. 

Lightning talk presentations will be 1 minute in length for a **single presentation slide**. The idea of the lightning talks is to introduce your poster in a 1 minute 1 slide presentation, to encourage longer discussions in front of your poster during the poster sessions.

Creativity is encouraged, and there will be prizes for:

- Best poster overall
- Most Creative Lightning Talk
- Best student poster

The poster must not exceed the size of A0 (1189-841mm) and be brought along to the Workshop for display. **Portrait format is preferred** to landscape, as our poster boards are slightly smaller than A0 landscape. All posters will be displayed for the duration of the Workshop. Poster presenters are required to attend the poster sessions.  

#### **Preparing your slides**

- Refer to the guidelines below, provided by the venue’s AV manager, when preparing your slides. This will help avoid technical problems on the day.

[Guidelines – Preparing Slides for AV](wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Preparing-Slides-for-AV-Videology.pdf)

- Name your presentation slides file **clearly**, using the following naming convention: **Your full name_Date_Session No_Start time****Example: *JohnSmith_19Aug_Session5_1630* (this will help us a lot to organise the slides!). If your talk is a lightning talk /poster please add the number of your poster at the end: *JohnSmith_19Aug_Session5_1630_Poster#***

- Upload your presentation directly to the Dropbox folder link sent to you through an email a week before the event by **midnight Monday 17 August (AEST).**
