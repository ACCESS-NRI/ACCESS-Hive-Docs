# Jasmeen Kaur

![Jasmeen Kaur](../wp-content/uploads/2023/04/latest-headshot-Jasmeen-300x300.jpg){ .align-right }

**Research Software Engineer, User Training Team**

Jasmeen is a research software engineer working within the ACCESS-NRI Training and Engagement team. Jasmeen’s favourite aspect of her work is being a part of an amazing team to collaboratively work on improving the understanding of the fundamental nature of climate and climate variability.

Jasmeen completed a Bachelor of Advanced Computing (Honours) in 2022 at The Australian National University.

Outside work, Jasmeen likes playing badminton, singing, and driving cars.
