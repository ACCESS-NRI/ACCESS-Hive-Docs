# Dr. Davide Marchegiani

![Dr. Davide Marchegiani](../wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Davide-BW-300x300.jpg){ .align-right }

**Senior Research Software Engineer**

Davide is a Senior Research Software Engineer working in the atmospheric development team on making ACCESS-CM2 and ACCESS-ESM1.5 experiments more systematically reproducible and user-friendly.

Favourite aspects of his work include the possibility to advance knowledge on climate science and high-performance computing, as well as contributing to the making of future climate scientists.

He has a PhD in Atmospheric Sciences from Monash University.

Outside work, Davide loves singing, playing piano and guitar, cooking, and socialising with friends.
