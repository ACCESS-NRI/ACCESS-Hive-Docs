# Corey Hanrahan

![Corey Hanrahan](../wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Profile-Picture-Corey-Hanrahan-BW-scaled-e1750046005746-300x300.png){ .align-right }

**Communications Officer**

Corey is a science communicator with a background in computational physics. His professional experience includes organising public science events, running educational science facility tours, performing inspirational science shows, and publishing high school textbooks. Corey brings his passion for learning and a strong attention to detail to ACCESS-NRI.

Corey completed a Bachelor of Advanced Science (Honours) in Physics at The University of Queensland, (2019) and a Master of Science Communication Outreach at Australian National University in 2020.

Corey likes spending his weekends and holidays in the outdoors doing hiking, canyoning, and caving. During the week, he enjoys cooking, reading, and playing board games
