# Felicity Chun

![Felicity Chun](../wp-content/uploads/2023/08/felicity-300x300.png){ .align-right }

**Senior Research Software Engineer**

Felicity’s role at ACCESS-NRI focuses on data in the Model Evaluation and Diagnostics team. The favourite aspect of her work is learning different technologies/software, new places and getting to the point where you have some understanding of the bigger picture.

Felicity has GIS work expertise in industry, including cartography, data collection/surveying and data analysis in Transport, Resources, Water industries.

She completed a Masters of IT (Spatial) at the University of Melbourne in 2018

Outside work, she loves taking her dog for a walk, playing sports including AFL, basketball and soccer, being outdoors in parks,beaches and hiking, eating good food.
