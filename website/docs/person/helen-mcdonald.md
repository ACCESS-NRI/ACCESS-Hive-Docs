# Dr. Helen Macdonald

![Dr. Helen Macdonald](../wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Helen-300x300.jpg){ .align-right }

**Team Lead, Regional & Coastal Ocean Modelling**

Helen is the Regional and Coastal Ocean Model Team Lead at ACCESS-NRI.

She has extensive experience modelling the ocean regionally in Australia, New Zealand and Antarctica. She loves the variety of applications that these models can be applied to, and the diverse range of people and specialisations she can collaborate with. Helen obtained her PhD in Physical Oceanography from UNSW in 2013.

Outside of work, Helen loves bush walking, skiing, and has recently started to sew.
