# Mika Peace

![Mika Peace](../wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Mika_headshot-BW-300x300.png){ .align-right }

**Fire weather researcher, High Impact Weather team,  Bureau of Meteorology**

Dr Mika Peace is a fire weather researcher in the High Impact Weather team at the [Australian Bureau of Meteorology](https://www.bom.gov.au/). She joined the Bureau in 2002 as an operational meteorologist and has worked in all states and territories across Australia in a range of roles in forecasting operations, training and commercial services and research as well as embedded in fire agencies.
