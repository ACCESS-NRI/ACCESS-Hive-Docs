# Tim Senden

![Tim Senden](../wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Tim-Senden-Cropped-BW-300x300.jpg){ .align-right }

**Board Member, Australian National University**

Professor Tim Senden FNAI, FAIP is an experimental physical chemist with experience in transdisciplinary research and commercialisation. He received his PhD in 1993 in Atomic Force Microscopy from the Australian National University (ANU), worked in France and the University of New South Wales (UNSW) before returning to ANU in 1997. He was the Director of the Research School of Physics for over 10 years before taking his current role as Pro Vice-Chancellor (Research Infrastructure and Entities).
