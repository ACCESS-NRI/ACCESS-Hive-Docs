# Dr. Mike Tetley

![Dr. Mike Tetley](../wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Mike_sm-BW-300x300.jpg){ .align-right }

**Team Lead, Ice Sheet Modelling**

Mike is the Team Leader of the new Ice Sheet Model Team at ACCESS-NRI.

Mike is focused on modelling workflow automation, diagnostic post-processing, and visualisation of model data. Mike’s favourite aspect of work is working with highly talented researchers and software engineers from around the world on scientific challenges that contribute to meaningful and positive societal change.

Outside work, Mike likes spending time with his family, mountain biking, climbing, trail running, travelling and playing drums.
