# Owen Kaluza

![Owen Kaluza](../wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Owen_photo-300x300.jpg){ .align-right }

**Senior Research Software Engineer, Visualisation Specialist**

Owen is working on developing high impact visualisations to better communicate the work done with access models and data, as well as produce/curate/release tools for researchers to better visualise, access and understand these datasets.

His favourite aspects of work are, finding ways to visualise, understand and interact with complex data, solving problems with creative software engineering and working with and learning from experts in all areas, especially science and research.

Owen attained a Bachelor of Computer Science at La Trobe University, and his current research involves building visualisation tools for use in Jupyter notebooks, 3D graphics with OpenGL, GPU programming, and python programming.

Outside of work, Owen enjoys cycling, gardening, hiking, camping and generative art.
