# Harshula Jayasuriya

![Harshula Jayasuriya](../wp-content/uploads/2022/10/harshula-BW-300x300.jpg){ .align-right }

**Principal Research Software Engineer**

Harshula is a Principal Research Software Engineer, working on improving build infrastructure and introducing industry best practices. The favourite aspect of his work is the opportunity to inject his skills and knowledge from the free/open source software domain into the science domain. Harshula is also the photographer and videographer of the ACCESS-NRI team.

Harshula obtained a Bachelor of Engineering (Computing) from the University of New South Wales and has been a Debian developer since 2012.

Outside work, he enjoys skiing and mountain biking.
