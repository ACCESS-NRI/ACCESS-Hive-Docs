# Tommy Gatti

![Tommy Gatti](../wp-content/uploads/2023/08/7b946f15-7e3b-4207-af9f-8dc4285edf1a-300x300.jpg){ .align-right }

**Senior Research Software Engineer**

Tommy is a Senior Research Software Engineer working in the Model Release Team at the ACCESS-NRI. He specialises in CI/CD and containerisation, and is currently working on CI/CD, Python Programming and DevOps.

Tommy completed a Bachelor of Advanced Computing (Honours) at the Australian National University, as well as a Bachelor of Arts in 2021.

Favourite aspects of his work are being able to research and investigate a wide variety of technical possibilities and increasing his skills in a variety of areas. Tommy also enjoys the team of people with which he works.

Outside of work, Tommy enjoys tinkering with small apps that can help me in my day-to-day, as well as gaming.
