# Rhaegar Zeng

![Rhaegar Zeng](../wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Rhaegar-Zeng-BW-300x300.jpg){ .align-right }

**Research Software Engineer**

Rhaegar is a research software engineer, working in the Model evaluation and diagnostics team. His goal is to help researchers and scientists to evaluate and diagnose models more easily and conveniently in the future.

Rhaegar has completed Bachelor of advanced computing (ANU) and a Master of Machine Learning and Computer Vision (ANU). His favourite aspect of work is meeting with experts from different countries, different fields and different backgrounds. In his free time, Rhaegar likes to watch movies, read some books, workout and kart racing.
