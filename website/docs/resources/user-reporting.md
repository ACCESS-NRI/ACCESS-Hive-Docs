# User Reporting

**Have you used ACCESS in recent or ongoing publications or projects? If so, we would love to know!**

![](../wp-content/uploads/2024/07/ACCESS-Structure-SOFTWARETOOLS-2022-11-30-300x300.png)

Reporting on the ACCESS models enables us to record how and where ACCESS is being used, and helps us to continue to be funded to deliver these services. We appreciate any information about the use of our models, tools and data. This is also a way for us to help you promote your work (if desired).

[Report your publication/project](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=XHJ941yrJEaa5fBTPkhkN2ZG6vIAVThPsNCAyezPHAVUQUtZOE4yVjhCSVkxQkpURzkyN1UxWTNKNS4u){ .buttononblue }

This information will be used to report on ACCESS-NRI’s impact to our funders. This **will not** include any personal information on individual responses collected.

If you have any questions about user reporting please contact ACCESS-NRI’s Communications and Engagement team at: [access.nri@anu.edu.au](mailto:access.nri@anu.edu.au)

Thank you for reporting the use of our infrastructure.
