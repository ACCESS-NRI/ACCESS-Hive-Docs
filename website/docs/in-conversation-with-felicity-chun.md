# In conversation with Felicity Chun

*July 29, 2026*

![](wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Felicity-Chun-Square-300x300.jpg)

Profile photo of Felicity Chun

In this article we speak with [Felicity Chun](person/felicity-chun.md), a Senior Research Software Engineer in ACCESS-NRI’s [Model Evaluation and Diagnostics (MED) team](teams/model-evaluation-and-diagnostics-med-team.md).

#### Where did you grow up, and how did you come to be part of the ACCESS-NRI team?

I grew up in Melbourne. I studied at the University of Melbourne and did a Bachelor of Environment majoring in Geomatics and later went back and did a Masters of IT in the spatial stream. While I was living in Mackay, Queensland, I saw an inviting ad and was interested in working closer to science and extending my software skills. I also wanted to move back a tad closer to Melbourne, despite the cold, so I applied and met some lovely people.

#### Tell us a bit about your career before joining ACCESS-NRI.

I liked the idea of working both indoors and outdoors and for my first graduate job I was a Mine Surveyor in an open pit mine in the Pilbara, Western Australia. I enjoyed the FIFO life from Perth for a few years before wanting to be more technical, and went back to Melbourne for a Masters of IT. In my final year I worked part time at a sort of startup called DAS (Digital Agriculture Services Pty Ltd) with some data wrangling and spatial tooling for their platform.

After graduating I decided to go hang out in Canada where I did an exchange semester and went back to doing survey work while I was there, gaining wisdom tidbits from a range of survey party chiefs. I came back to Australia for warmer weather and lived in sunny Queensland doing GIS project work as part of the environment and planning team in engineering consulting.

#### What do you do at ACCESS-NRI, and what excites you about this work?

I’m part of the [Model Evaluation and Diagnostics (MED) team](teams/model-evaluation-and-diagnostics-med-team.md) at ACCESS-NRI where I assist with evaluating and comparing model outputs with other datasets including other CMIP data and observations. I collaborate internationally with Research Software Engineers on developing and maintaining [ESMValTool](https://esmvaltool.org/), which is an Earth System Model evaluation software package and with Obs4MIPs panel which collates observational datasets for use in model intercomparison projects. In my role, I am also part of the [ACCESS-Hive Docs](https://docs.access-hive.org.au/) team to help maintain that documentation website. I am constantly learning in this role, and I like to assist a range of people, including in my team, recently as part of some visualisation projects.

#### What do you like to do outside of work?

I have been playing some community AFL which I started in Queensland – this forces me to run around and be outside in winter and meet a lovely group of women. Otherwise, I really enjoy eating, even more with friends. I also love teasing and exercising my dog named Patrick.
