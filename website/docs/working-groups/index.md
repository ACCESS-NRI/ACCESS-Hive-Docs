# Community Working Groups (CWG)

**Community Working Groups** are formed by users and developers of specific ACCESS components, configurations or research communities. They aim to:

- link the different research communities and the ACCESS-NRI infrastructure experts
- manage and share information and experiences about model configurations within that specific research community
- give advice on the optimal allocation of available ACCESS-NRI computational resources
- be open, inclusive, supportive and flexible.
