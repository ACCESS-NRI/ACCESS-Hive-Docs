# Cryospheric Sciences Working Group

## About

![](../wp-content/uploads/2023/03/ACCESS-icon-CRYOSPHERE-Title-281x300.png)

The Cryospheric Sciences Working Group includes researchers from Australian universities and research institutions who use and co-develop the Australian Community Climate Earth System Model (ACCESS) for their research.

It includes the components of the cryosphere with a focus on land ice, including ice sheets, ice shelves, alpine glaciers, snow, permafrost, fast ice and icebergs.

[How to join a Community Working Group](../join-community-working-groups.md){ .buttononblue }

[Cryospheric Sciences Working Group home](https://forum.access-hive.org.au/c/cryosphere/working-group/45){ .buttononblue }

Click on the “Cryospheric Sciences Working Group home” button above to follow this working group mailing list, information about events and meetings and activity reports.

#### Aims

The main aims of this group are to co-develop the cryosphere and land ice models, interchange information through regular meetings, and provide information about training.
