# Machine Learning for Climate and Weather Working Group

## About

![](../wp-content/uploads/2024/08/ACCESS-icon-SOFTWARE-300x300.png)

The Machine Learning for Climate and Weather Working Group includes researchers from several Australian universities and research institutions who use and co-develop the Australian Community Climate Earth System Model (ACCESS) for their research.

[How to join a Community Working Group](../join-community-working-groups.md){ .buttononblue }

[Machine learning for Climate and Weather Working Group Home](https://forum.access-hive.org.au/t/about-the-machine-learning-for-climate-and-weather-working-group/3662){ .buttononblue }

Click on the “Machine Learning for Climate and Weather Working Group home” button above to follow this working group mailing list, information about events and meetings and activity reports.

#### Aims

The main aims of this group are to:

- facilitate mutually-beneficial collaborations between climate/weather scientists and ML experts
- create a platform for knowledge exchange where ACCESS-NRI, NCI, the climate community and ML experts share insights, data, and methodologies to accelerate progress in both fields.
- advise ACCESS-NRI, NCI and the climate community on advances in ML in climate/weather science and techniques and their impact on future directions in climate/weather research (e.g. modelling, resourcing etc.)
- ensure that the Australian community remains at the forefront of the use of ML/AI in research and applications in weather and climate
- contribute to the training of climate/weather scientists in applying ML to their research (through organised events/ resource sharing).
