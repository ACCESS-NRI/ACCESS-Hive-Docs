# Models Credits/Acknowledgements

**CASA: Carnegie-Ames-Stanford Approach (CASA).** Original publication: Field, C. B., Randerson, J. T., & Malmström, C. M. (1995). Global net primary production: Combining ecology and remote sensing. Remote Sensing of Environment, 51(1), 74-88.

**CABLE: Community Atmosphere Biosphere Land Exchange Model**  Developed by CSIRO

<https://cable.readthedocs.io/en/latest/user_guide/>

Original publication: Kowalczyk, E.A., Wang, Y.P., Law, R.M., Davies, H.L., McGregor, J.L. and Abramowitz,G.S. 2006. The CSIRO Atmosphere Biosphere Land Exchange (CABLE) model for use in climate models and as an offline model. (CSIRO Marine and Atmospheric Research Paper;013) Aspendale, Vic.: CSIRO Marine and Atmospheric Research. 43p.

**UM: Unified Model (UM). Met Office, UK:** <https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/research/approach/modelling-systems/unified-model>

**UKCA: United Kingdom Chemistry and Aerosols (UKCA) Model**

<https://www.ukca.ac.uk/wiki/index.php/UKCA>

Original paper: Morgenstern, O., et al. (2009). “The UKCA Chemistry and Aerosol Model.” *Geoscientific Model Development*, 2(2), 1-23.

**CMEPS/CDEPS: Community Mediator for Earth Prediction Systems (CMEPS) and Community Data and Evaluation Prediction System (CDEPS)**

This coupling infrastructure is a joint development of a number of groups including NCAR, NOAA/EMC, and NOAA/GFDL

**ISSM: Ice Sheet System Model**

ISSM is an ice sheet model developed by the NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) and the University of California, Irvine (UCI), [https://issm.jpl.nasa.gov/](https://science.jpl.nasa.gov/projects/issm/)

**CICE:   The Los Alamos sea ice model** . Original publication: <https://zenodo.org/record/1205674>

**MOM6: Modular Ocean Model**

Original publication: Adcroft, A., et al. (2019). ” **The GFDL Global Ocean and Sea Ice Model OM4.0: Model Description and Simulation Features**.” *Journal of Advances in Modeling Earth Systems*, 11(10). Link: <https://doi.org/10.1029/2019MS001726>

**MOM5:** *Griffies et al. (2012) Elements of the Modular Ocean Model, NOAA/GFDL Technical Report*

**WOMBAT:** **World Ocean Model of Biogeochemistry And Trophic-dynamics**
