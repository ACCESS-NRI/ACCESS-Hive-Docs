# ACCESS-NRI releases its Work Plan for 2026–2027

*July 2, 2026*

Ever wondered what ACCESS-NRI is working on and how it may benefit the ACCESS community this year? 

ACCESS-NRI has released our [**Annual Work Plan**](wp-content/uploads/2026/07/ACCESS-NRI-Work-Plan-FY2026-27.pdf) for the 2026**–**2027 financial year.

![](wp-content/uploads/2026/07/WORKPLAN-2026-2027-Report-Cover-212x300.png){ .align-right }The 2026–2027 Work Plan details projects under four broad focus areas:   

- Model Development
- Model Releases & Infrastructure
- Software & Data
- Training & Engagement

Our Annual Work Plan is intended to provide transparency about our work and to promote feedback and discussion with our community.

ACCESS-NRI would like to thank all the people who contributed ideas and feedback throughout the development of the Work Plan. Your input has helped to shape the activities and priorities outlined in this years’ plan
