# COSIMA Hackathon program for ACCESS Community Workshop 2025

The 2025 COSIMA Hackathon is held on Monday 8 September to Tuesday 9 September at [Melbourne Connect](https://maps.app.goo.gl/UvpqeX9QjVxGjvA47).

The program is subject to amendments—please revisit this webpage for the most up-to-date version of the program. For more information, including this year’s dress up theme and background on the COSIMA Cookbook Recipes, visit the [COSIMA 2-day Hackathon v5.0 topic on the ACCESS-Hive Forum](https://forum.access-hive.org.au/t/cosima-2-day-hackathon-v5-0-monday-sep-8-tuesday-sep-9-2025/4794).

- [Day 2: Tuesday 9 September](#Day-2)
