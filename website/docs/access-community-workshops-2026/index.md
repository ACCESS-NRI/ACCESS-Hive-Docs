# ACCESS Community Workshops 2026

As decided at the 2025 ACCESS Community Workshop, ACCESS-NRI is supporting three smaller, targeted ACCESS Community Workshops this year, each organised by two of the ACCESS Community Working Groups. Everyone is welcome to each of these workshops. In 2027, we will once again hold one combined ACCESS Community Workshop.

In 2026, the three workshops are:

---

#### ACCESS COSIMA & Cryosphere Community Workshop

![ACCESS COSIMA & Cryosphere Workshop, Nipaluna/Hobart, 16–20 November, 2026](../wp-content/uploads/2026/05/COSIMA-Cryosphere-Workshop-2026-Post-3200x1800-1-1024x576.png)

**When:** 16–20 November 2026
**Where:** Nipaluna/Hobart, Australia

This workshop aims to facilitate community connection and genuine collaboration between the cryospheric sciences and ocean/sea ice modelling and observational communities. It is organised by the [COSIMA](../working-groups/cosima.md) and [Cryospheric Sciences](../working-groups/cryosphere.md) Community Working Groups.

**Extended registrations close Wednesday 4 November!**

[Workshop webpage](cosima-cryosphere.md){ .buttononblue } [Register](https://events.humanitix.com/access-cosima-cryosphere-workshop){ .buttononblue } [Program](cosima-cryosphere/program.md){ .buttononblue }

---

#### ACCESS Community Workshop on Land and Coupled Modelling

![](../wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Land-and-Global-Workshop-2026-Website3-300x169.png)

**When:** 31 August – 2 September, 2026
**Where:** Naarm/Melbourne, Australia

This workshop aims to strengthen the ACCESS research community, improve ACCESS modelling capability, and support the ACCESS CMIP7 submission. It is organised by the [Land Surface Modelling](../land-surface-modelling-community-working-group.md) and [Earth System Modelling](../earth-system-modelling-working-group.md) Community Working Groups.

[Workshop webpage](../access-community-workshop-on-land-and-coupled-modelling.md){ .buttononblue }

---

#### ACCESS Community Workshop Connecting Machine Learning to Earth System Science

![](../wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Machine-Learning-Workshop-2026-Website1-300x169.png)

**When:** 19–21 August, 2026
**Where:** Naarm/Melbourne, Australia

This workshop aims to bridge the gap between the vast amounts of complex data generated by Earth system sciences and the powerful new machine learning tools. It is organised by the [Machine Learning for Weather and Climate](../working-groups/machine-learning.md) and [Atmosphere](../working-groups/atmosphere.md) Community Working Groups, and will include a day of meetings for both working groups.

[Workshop webpage](../access-workshop-2026-connecting-machine-learning-to-earth-system-science.md){ .buttononblue }

---

We encourage everyone to attend, especially students and early career researchers.
