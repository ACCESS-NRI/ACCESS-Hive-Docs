# Model Release team

![](../wp-content/uploads/2025/09/ACCESS-icon-RELEASE-RGB-e1756786149391-150x150.png)

## About the team

The ACCESS-NRI Model Release team is responsible for building, maintaining, and testing climate and weather models automatically, ensuring they are ready when needed. This is crucial for enabling teams to work more productively and for users to effectively utilise the software we develop.

Our team focuses on several key areas:

- Automated code integration and testing
- Resource tracking
- Continuous integration testing and deployment
- Model configuration release and testing

#### Engage with us

If you’re interested in learning more about our work or collaborating with us, we invite you to contact our team or reach out through the [ACCESS-Hive Forum](https://forum.access-hive.org.au/). Your feedback is invaluable as we strive to improve our systems and support the research community effectively.
