# User Training team

![](../wp-content/uploads/2023/06/ACCESS-icon-TRAINING-e1686717448630-150x150.png)

## About the team

Training helps researchers to use our tools and models, so they can focus on their research. Our team works with technical experts at ACCESS-NRI and across the ACCESS research community to strengthen community skills and expertise.

Our training covers the full spectrum of ACCESS users and scenarios, from new research students who are learning fundamentals and best practices, to experts who are developing the next generations of models, tools and data, and taking on the most challenging research problems.

This includes:

- Overseeing and providing training on ACCESS Models and tools
- Creating online training resources and supporting other teams to produce them
- Providing documentation support
- Supporting users through the User support scheme

#### Engage with us

We are always looking for opportunities to innovate and improve our training, and to collaborate with the community through courses, self-guided modules, hackathons, workshops, and conferences. If you have ideas about training or unmet requirements we would love to hear more and discuss possibilities.
