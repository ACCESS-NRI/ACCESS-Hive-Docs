# ACCESS-NRI releases its Work Plan for 2025–2026

*July 15, 2025*

Ever wondered what ACCESS-NRI is working on and how it may benefit the ACCESS community this year? 

ACCESS-NRI has released our **[Annual Work Plan](wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Work-Plan-FY2025-26-1.pdf)** for the 2025**–**2026 financial year.

The 2025–2026 Work Plan details projects under four broad focus areas:   

- Model Development
- Model Releases & Infrastructure
- Software & Data
- Training & Engagement

The Work Plan was guided by the [ACCESS-NRI Strategic Plan (2022](about/access-nri-strategic-plan-2022-2027.md)[**–**](wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Work-Plan-FY2025-26.pdf)[2027)](about/access-nri-strategic-plan-2022-2027.md) and reflects priorities that emerged over ACCESS-NRI’s establishment period. This included community feedback from the 2022, 2023 and 2024 ACCESS Community Workshops and input from our Community Working Groups, Scientific Advisory Committee and Advisory Board.  

Our Annual Work Plan is intended to provide transparency about our work and to promote feedback and discussion with our community. Please visit the [ACCESS-Hive Forum](https://forum.access-hive.org.au/t/draft-work-plan-fy2025-26/4524) if you would like to access all the input provided by the community in the last months.
